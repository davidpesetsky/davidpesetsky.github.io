For over three decades, I have been principal second violin in the Newton (MA)-based <a href="https://www.newphil.org"><strong>New Philharmonia Orchestra</strong></a> and more recently also play in the violin sections of the <a href="https://www.brooklinesymphony.org"><strong>Brookline Symphony</strong></a>. I get great pleasure from playing chamber music as well.  For the last five summers, I have attended the wonderful <a href="https://cmceast.org"><strong>CMC East</strong></a> chamber music conference at Colgate as a violinist and violist. Three years ago, I also decided to take violin lessons again, after a gap of exactly 50 years. My teacher is <a href="https://www.perezespejo.com"><strong>Miguel Pérez-Espejo Cárdenas</strong></a> — who turned out to be the perfect teacher for me. I have learned more about music and the violin from these lessons than I could ever have imagined possible.
